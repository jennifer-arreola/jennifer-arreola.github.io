About

Hi there! My name is Jennifer and I'm a data scientist with a particular interest in business analytics. My path into data started with a curiosity for finding the story behind the numbers. I enjoy the moment a dataset stops being noise and starts pointing to more clear decisions. That's carried me from statistical modeling in R to building full pipelines with Airflow and dbt, to dashboards that put insights directly in front of stakeholders.

I'm especially drawn to problems at the intersection of forecasting and operations, but truly all business processes intrigue me. I strive to facilitate the kind of work where a model has a visible, real-world payoff. My toolkit spans SQL, Python, R, and Power BI, with a focus on machine learning and time series forecasting.
