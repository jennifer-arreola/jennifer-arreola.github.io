About

I'm Jennifer Arreola, a data scientist focused on business analytics. My path into data started with a curiosity for finding the story behind the numbers — I love the moment a dataset stops being noise and starts pointing to a clear decision. That's carried me from statistical modeling in R to building full pipelines with Airflow and dbt, to dashboards that put insights directly in front of stakeholders.

I'm especially drawn to problems at the intersection of forecasting and operations — the kind of work where a good model has a visible, real-world payoff.
